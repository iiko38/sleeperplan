# Worked outputs

Generated with Sleeperplan 0.1.0 and the dated Wickes catalogue, as of 6 September 2026.
These are hypothetical dimensions, not a survey of the neighbour's beds.
All packs deliberately remain DRAFT_MARK_OUT_ONLY: fixing pilots and site/connection details require review.

- `height-options/height-comparison.pdf`: same-scale two- and three-course comparison.
- `height-options/2-courses/workshop.pdf`: full 400 mm high example pack.
- `height-options/3-courses/workshop.pdf`: full 600 mm high example pack.
- `batch/`: three beds optimised as one cutting and purchasing job.
- `with-offcut/`: an existing measured sleeper/offcut included in the allocation.

The 2,400 x 1,200 mm outside footprint has a 2,200 x 1,000 mm clear opening.
Two courses: 6 purchased sleepers, 6 saw cuts, 30 fixing entries, GBP 189.30 timber and screws.
Three courses: 9 purchased sleepers, 8 saw cuts, 52 fixing entries, GBP 294.60 timber and screws.
Those are not complete installation prices: unresolved costs are explicitly unpriced in each plan.

See the main README for commands to generate a new job. Do not modify generated outputs to change a design; edit the input and regenerate into a new directory.
